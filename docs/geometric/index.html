Diameter

The diamater of a set of points is the rectangle enclosed by min x,max x ;min y ,max y in which these points lie in.The diamter is the length of such rectangle.

Enclosing circle

Enclosing circle(a circle covering all points) is found by drawing a circle with the diameter being the max of the extreme x and y points. For most of points this is also the minimum enclosing circle.

Closest Pair Of Points

The closest pair of points are adjacent in x sorted and y sorted pair of points. The minimum of these is the true closest pair.

Runtime Complexity

Diameter: O(n)

Circle: O(n)

Closest Pair: O(nlogn)

boundary/convex/polygon: O(nlogn)

* Where n = number of points

The Challenge

Computing rules for boundary inclusion/exclusion is computationally intensive — O(nxn) for points greater than three. The real challenge lies in non-favourable distribuion of points due to randomness and and self contradicting nature of simple comparison based methods.Inefficient sorting is entirely educational and the most convenient tool,but sorting is rarely even a factor here , unlike say comparing two furthest points when looking for closest pair(a waste mostly).

My approach: Minimize unnecessary computation through simple array and comparison checks.

My Approach

Instead of naive bruteforce, I use:

  • Simple min/max checks:Diamter is the max of the to extremes
  • Level two pruning:Comapre adjacent points as candidates for closest pair instead of quadratic time or lower to check adjacency and closesnes
  • Sort as the cost to pay:Most rules and onservations are easier to implement and prove after sorting
  • Memory efficiency: Minimize temporary allocations

Different Strategies

Naive O(nxn) Ineffient sorting step before proceeding or just straight bruteforce one point for all other points .
Improved O(nlogn) Better sorting but with more perfomance overhead
Clever O(nlogn) Optimized sort and steps touching the absolute minimum in all operations with better cache locaity
Elite O(nlogn) but parallel Massive parallelism, 100x+ speedup
